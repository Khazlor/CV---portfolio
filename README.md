# Experience / Qualifications

### 3D Graphic

In the past, I have actively made 3D models for characters, environments, and many other objects in Blender for about 8 years. These models were both for games and for 3D printing. I also handled other aspects related to 3D models, such as materials and textures, normal maps, animations, simplified collisions, etc. I also have experience with programs like Substance Painter, ZBrush, and technical 3D modeling in SolidWorks.

I primarily exported these 3D models to the Unreal Engine 4 (versions 4.14 - 4.25), where I then worked on their implementation into my personal game project.

### Programmer

Regarding programming, I have extensive experience with both OOP and non-OOP languages. At university, we programmed many different projects, ranging from basic CLI programs to applications, compilers, and interpreters.

I have experience with languages such as C/C++, Java, Python, Pascal, and GDScript. For the past year and a half, I have been actively programming in GDScript, but after a brief reminder of the syntax, transitioning to another language or learning a new one should not be a problem. I am currently learning C#.

For the last year and a half, I have been actively working on a large gaming project called Open-VTT in the Godot 4 game engine. It is an open-source 2D virtual tabletop for playing tabletop role-playing games such as Dungeons and Dragons, Pathfinder, and many others. More at: https://github.com/Khazlor/Open-VTT

As part of it, I have worked on the entire game structure, designed improvements over current market solutions, UI, lighting system, user input processing, evaluation of user-entered commands, multiplayer, undo/redo system, file uploading and sharing during gameplay, saving and loading maps and characters, a tool for converting PDF forms with character information used in tabletop games into a format usable for the VTT, linking characters with their tokens, targeting system, automatic action evaluation, and much more.
Thus, I have certain experience in multiple areas of game development, but I do not specialize in any.

If you would like to get to know my thought process while creating the project, most of it is described in czech in my bachelor's thesis, the result of which is Open-VTT: https://www.vut.cz/studenti/zav-prace/detail/156289
